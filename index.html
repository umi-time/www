Umi‑Time

A solar‑aligned time system based on degrees of rotation, not civil clock hours.

What is Umi‑Time?

Umi‑Time is a planetary time standard that represents the passage of a day as 360 degrees of rotation, aligned to the Sun rather than to local civil clock conventions. It provides a unified way to describe time across locations, seasons, and cultures using a single continuous scale.
